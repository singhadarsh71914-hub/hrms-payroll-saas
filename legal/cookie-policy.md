# Cookie Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-06-28

This Cookie Policy explains how we use cookies and similar tracking technologies on our platform.

## 1. Strictly Necessary Cookies
We use cookies essential for authentication and security.
- **Authentication:** We issue highly secure, `HttpOnly`, `Secure`, and `SameSite=Strict` cookies to manage user sessions (JWT Refresh Tokens).
- **Security:** CSRF tokens and rate-limiting trackers to protect the platform.

## 2. Non-Essential Cookies
We currently **do not** use non-essential tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or third-party behavioral trackers. Our analytics (if any) are fully anonymized.

## 3. Managing Cookies
Because our cookies are strictly necessary for the platform to function (logging in, session persistence), disabling them via your browser will render the HRMS application unusable.
