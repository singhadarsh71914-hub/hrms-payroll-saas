# Getting Started

## 1. Initial Login
When your organization is provisioned, the primary Administrator will receive an email containing a temporary access link.
1. Click the link in the welcome email.
2. You will be redirected to the **Set Password** screen.
3. Enter a secure password (minimum 8 characters).
4. Log in using your email and the newly created password.

## 2. Verify Your Email
Upon logging in for the first time, you will see a blue banner at the top of the dashboard:
> "Please verify your email address to unlock full platform features."

1. Click **Verify Now**.
2. Check your inbox for the verification link and click it.
3. The platform will confirm verification and unlock Payroll, Document Management, and API access.

## 3. The Dashboard
Your Admin Dashboard is divided into three main sectors:
- **Left Sidebar:** Navigation for HR Core, Finance, and Compliance modules.
- **Top Bar:** Global Search and Notifications (the Bell icon).
- **Main View:** Quick widgets showing Pending Leaves, Today's Attendance, and active Headcount.

**Next Step:** Proceed to [Company Setup](./company-setup.md) to configure your organizational hierarchy.
