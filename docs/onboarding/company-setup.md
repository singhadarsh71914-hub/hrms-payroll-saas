# Company Setup

Before inviting employees, you must define the organizational structure and legal parameters.

## 1. Statutory Information
Navigate to **Settings > Company Settings**.
1. Enter the legal **Company Name** (as registered with authorities).
2. Input the **PAN** (Permanent Account Number) and **TAN** (Tax Deduction Account Number) if operating in India.
3. Add the **Registered Address**. This address will automatically print on all generated Payslips and Form 16 documents.

## 2. Departments
Navigate to **Settings > Departments**.
1. Click **+ Add Department**.
2. Enter the department name (e.g., `Engineering`, `Sales`).
3. *(Optional)* Select a Department Head. Note: The Head must already exist as an employee in the system.

## 3. Designations (Titles)
Navigate to **Settings > Designations**.
1. Click **+ Add Designation**.
2. Create standard job titles (e.g., `Software Engineer II`, `Regional Manager`).
3. These titles will be available in the dropdown when onboarding new employees.
