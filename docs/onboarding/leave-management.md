# Leave Management Setup

## 1. Defining Leave Types
1. Go to **Settings > Leave Types**.
2. Common defaults (Sick, Casual, Earned) are pre-populated.
3. To add a new type (e.g., Maternity Leave), click **+ Add Leave Type**.
4. Set the annual quota (e.g., 26 weeks for Maternity in India).
5. Determine if this leave is Paid or Unpaid. Unpaid leaves automatically trigger LOP (Loss of Pay) deductions during payroll processing.

## 2. Leave Accrual & Balances
- Leave balances are credited to employees on their `date_of_joining` on a pro-rata basis for the current financial year.
- Earned leaves can be configured to carry forward to the next year, while Casual/Sick leaves generally lapse on December 31st (or March 31st for the Indian financial year).

## 3. The Approval Workflow
1. Employee navigates to **My Leaves** and submits a request.
2. The direct `Reporting Manager` receives an email notification and a dashboard alert.
3. The Manager reviews the team calendar to prevent understaffing and clicks **Approve** or **Reject**.
4. Once approved, the days are deducted from the employee's quota and automatically reflected as `ON LEAVE` in the Attendance logs.
